# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Antibody to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Sharing other people's session transcripts, logs, or credentials.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainers at **CONDUCT_CONTACT_TBD**. Reports are handled confidentially. If a maintainer is involved, you can report to any other maintainer directly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review every report and may respond, depending on severity, with:

1. **A private warning** explaining what was wrong.
2. **A temporary ban** from project spaces.
3. **A permanent ban** for repeated or severe violations, including harassment.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed from their role.
